## Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 2 cups cold water

## Directions
1. strain pulp from lemon juice, add to pitcher
2. add sugar, stir
3. add 2 cups of water
4. add up to 1 more cup of water to taste or fill glass 2/3 - 3/4 with lemonade base and top with water or seltzer
